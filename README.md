# Kirsov – Smart Payment Tracking System

## Overview

Kirsov is a business payment tracking platform designed to help small businesses, schools, freelancers, and organizations manage customer payments, reminders, and payment history from a single dashboard.

The goal is to replace manual payment tracking with an organized digital workflow.

---

## Problem

Many businesses still manage payments manually using notebooks, spreadsheets, or messaging apps. This causes:

- Lost payment records
- Missed payment deadlines
- Manual reminder sending
- Difficulty tracking payment history
- Poor customer management

---

## Solution

Kirsov centralizes payment management into one system with reminders, notices, payment history, and progress tracking.

---

## Features

- Customer Management
